Fig. I  —  The stack, separated

Four layers. One system.

Outsole, midsole, upper, lacing — each layer of the VX-1 was designed on its own, then tuned as a single machine. Keep scrolling and watch it come apart along the stack.

Fig. II  —  Outsole

Grip that bites back.

A full-length rubber compound mapped to the pressure path of a footstrike — more rubber where you land, less where you don't. Wet roads included.

  • 3.2 mm lugs
  • Wet grip rated
  • Full length

Fig. III  —  Midsole

Foam that returns the favour.

Nitrogen-infused foam compresses on landing and gives the energy straight back on toe-off. Soft is slow — this foam is neither.

  • 85% energy return
  • 38 mm stack
  • N₂ infused

Fig. IV  —  Upper & lacing

A sock that thinks it's a shoe.

One-piece AeroKnit wraps the midfoot like a second skin, and the lacing system locks the heel without a single pressure point. Nothing flaps, nothing slips.

  • 1-piece knit
  • 0 slip at heel
  • 212 g total
